WHAT WAS FIXED
- Large Area Wise and 350-page Party Wise reports no longer run one massive synchronous matching loop.
- Matching now runs in small browser-friendly batches.
- Product matching is indexed and cached, so repeated medicines are resolved once.
- The same report import replaces its previous copy, preventing browser storage from growing on every test.
- Admin unmatched tables are capped at 200 visible rows to protect the page. Use "Download all as CSV" for the complete saved list.

DATA NOTES
