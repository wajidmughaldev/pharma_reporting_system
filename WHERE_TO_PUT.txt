WHAT CHANGED
- A unique product-name match no longer requires TP to match.
- Trailing report labels such as TOTAL and TTL are removed from product names.
- Strong name matches of 90% or more are assigned by name when only one company is possible.
- TP is used only to resolve a strong-name match when multiple companies remain possible.
- Lower-confidence fuzzy matches and genuine extra form/pack differences can still require TP.
- This applies to both Area Wise and Party Wise imports.
